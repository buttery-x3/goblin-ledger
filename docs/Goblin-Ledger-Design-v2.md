# Goblin Ledger — dashboard design

**Reviewed edition · 19 November 2026 · Local Windows web application**  
Companion: [Agent development plan](Goblin-Ledger-Agent-Plan-v2.md)

## The decision the dashboard exists to support

**What could I do, with the characters I have or could level, to make gold on my PvP Alliance auction house?** The player might go gathering, run a dungeon, level a profession, sell a reagent to people following a popular guide, or sit on an item with an unusual use. A market whose price barely moves can be the most interesting market on the screen.

The product is a dense, configurable research terminal for an experienced WoW player. The Atlas compares geographically actionable opportunities across the world. Opportunities scans the entire house, including items with no useful location. The player uses one account and one home market. Region remains unconfirmed. Let the player choose it once and display the resolved region, PvP ruleset and Alliance house together. A profile works before the addon is installed.

### What the review changed

| Issue in the first design | Decision for this edition |
|---|---|
| A flat price, units listed at that price and a replenishing wall could be read as one finding. | Publish each finding separately with the observations that qualify it. Exact-tier history starts at the first qualifying observation. |
| A colour for each zone implied that credible gold/hour already exists everywhere. | Make sourced routes available immediately. Colour comparable routes when yield and price evidence permits it. Show break-even yield and missing inputs for the rest. |
| “Next Legacy point” could recommend the same challenge twice. | Track completed challenges at account scope and selected perks per character. Treat unknown account completion as unknown. |
| A positive one-craft margin could imply a profitable batch or repayment of a recipe unlock. | Quote the chosen batch against the actual input tiers. Price the alternative use of owned materials, the available demand evidence and the marginal benefit of the unlock. |
| A failed source probe could pass a live-data milestone. | Separate a working failure path from evidence that the integration delivered usable data. The development plan gives each its own test and release status. |
| The public test plan used an actual reported personal holding. | Public accounting examples below use synthetic quantities. The player’s ledger and exported account data stay local. |

## 1. Workspace and the linked Atlas

The initial workspace has a **full-height Atlas** on the left, about 58% of the width, and **Farms, Opportunities and Characters** stacked on the right. The split is adjustable. Offer single-pane, side-by-side, one-plus-three and eight-pane presets. Every viewport can hold a different tool or a tab group. Save named arrangements with split sizes, tab order, filters, sorting, density, active overlay, map extent and selections. Maximise/restore preserves the arrangement.

Use compact headers, 12–13 px adjustable table text, tabular gold/silver/copper figures, sticky column headings and aligned sparklines. Charts expose their units, time windows and source timestamps. Panel widths decide which subordinate columns collapse. The workspace header carries the house, live/demo state, freshness and a shortcut to Console. Table search, keyboard selection and a route list give access to anything the map can select.

**Atlas layers.** Use flat vector continent outlines with literal coloured areas. Start with estimated net g/h and change in net g/h. Offer guide exposure and observation coverage as separate overlays. Use one legend per active metric with a time window, comparable scale and a distinct hatch for missing data. Filters include farm type, class/profession eligibility, session length and evidence basis. With no imported character, show routes and requirements across the world. A personal overlay can be enabled after the player selects a manual or imported profile.

| Scale or interaction | Required behaviour |
|---|---|
| World / zone | For g/h, colour a zone by its highest qualifying repeatable route under the selected filters. Tooltip names that route, number of qualifying routes, yield evidence and age of the price. For change, compute each route’s like-for-like change first and identify the route contributing the zone value. Changes in which route leads must be visible. |
| Route / instance | A gathering area is a patch; a circuit is a line or corridor. Selection outlines only that route. A dungeon uses a world entrance and a separate interior footprint when a floor is mapped. Include meaningful Forever-specific areas and allow insets for disconnected maps. A zone-only record can appear in the list with its precision stated. |
| Click a farm hotspot | Resolve its stable **farm ID**, not its herb or zone. Select and scroll to it in the most recently active visible Farms pane. Otherwise bring an existing Farms tab forward. Otherwise use the next empty viewport in layout order. |
| Workspace is full | Open a Farms tab in the most recently active side group, falling back to the first side group. If the Atlas occupies the only group, create a right split. Keep the Atlas visible. If the target group is hosting Atlas and Farms as sibling tabs, move/show Farms in a side group so both remain visible. |
| Filters / repeated clicks | Temporarily reveal a selected farm hidden by pane filters. Preserve the original filter and offer return. Repeated clicks reuse the pane. Reverse selection highlights the farm without re-emitting a navigation loop. Characters highlights eligible alts. Opportunities keeps its house-wide selection and sort unless the player explicitly links it. |

**Geometry provenance.** A zone rectangle from `UiMapAssignment` locates a map frame; it cannot supply its irregular boundary. A source listing nodes in a zone supplies neither a circuit nor a harvest rate. Store the map ID, continent/instance/floor, coordinate units, transform, geometry precision, build and source on each route. For known shapes verify anchors across the extent, including axis direction. Ship original schematic or redistributable vector geometry. Where only an entrance or approximate locality is supported, render it as such. ## 3. An opportunity engine that can explain its results

### A flat market is a first-class signal

A published **US PvP Alliance** history for Dark Iron Residue gives a useful regression case. Three chart observations on 13 November 2026, at 06:00, 15:00 and 21:00 UTC, show a **1s lowest ask** alongside quantities of **6,522**, **2,478** and **3,165**. These are observations from the published history. They illustrate what a movers table misses. The quantities refer to that chart’s listed quantity; they do not disclose how many units sat at 1s or who supplied them. This is a historical example, independent of which region the player ultimately selects. [Residue price history](https://bootybaybroker.com/forever/item/18945/dark-iron-residue) · [Forever vendor value](https://outof.games/realms/forever/item/18945-dark-iron-residue/)

| Finding | Minimum evidence shown in the row |
|---|---|
| **Pinned to vendor** | Compare the lowest valid ask with a dated vendor sell value. A starting scanner preset is 8 expected hourly slots, at least 6 distinct valid observations, and equality in at least 75% of those observed. Show both ratios. Deduplicate timestamps. These thresholds are tunable product defaults. |
| **Concentrated floor** | Exact floor units and share of listed supply at the same snapshot. Initial bulk-commodity preset: at least 100 units and at least 50% of all units at the floor. Show next tier and meaningful depth beyond a tiny second listing. The exact first tier can also be inferred when the next tier is **strictly** above 105% of the ask and the documented 5% depth is complete. Otherwise request the item book. |
| **Observed refill** | Time-ordered exact floor quantities at comparable consecutive observations show a fall followed by a rise. Show both deltas and chart the coverage. A gap breaks the sequence. Hourly observations can miss intra-hour activity. A refill establishes a change in displayed supply; seller intent remains a hypothesis. |
| **Possible new use** | A source identifies a relationship and its provenance. Quest 7737 has a Forever-labelled page carrying Classic details; the source also says its client list omits the quest. Keep the repeatable turn-in as a watch thesis pending current observation of the hand-in. Evidence for vendor value and for future demand has separate status. [Quest provenance](https://www.60.tools/quests/7737-gaining-acceptance) |

Sweep all eligible bulk rows before consulting watches or inventory. Queue exact-depth checks for promising candidates with bounded frequency. A single 1s auction above an otherwise expensive book qualifies as a price lead at most. A frequent 1s ask plus a dense tier qualifies as a wall. Both can appear even when price change is 0%. Flag a below-vendor buy only for actually purchasable units, with verified per-unit sell value, batch cost and item restrictions. At parity, an Alliance AH sale can realise less gold than vendoring after the sale cut. [Market fields and inference](https://auctionhouse.tk.gg/data/) · [Observed auction fees](https://wowfah.com/guides/wow-forever-auction-house-cut-and-deposit)

### How the scanner finds unfamiliar plays

| Discovery | Candidates and evidence to inspect |
|---|---|
| **A BoE worth a second look** | Index tradeable gear with a changed effect, scarce resistance combination or few comparable substitutes. Join current asks to prior observations at the same item/variant resolution. A distinctive effect alone earns a “use case” lead. An unusually low ask against comparable, sufficiently recent trade evidence earns a price-discrepancy lead. Show how many observations support the comparison. An old listing that disappears carries much weaker evidence than a player receipt. |
| **An alternative to a publicised farm** | Start at the named route and its output. Find the other sources of that output, their requirements and a route-specific yield estimate. Compare session returns and recent changes. Advice about one cave can surface a second cave for investigation even before there is enough evidence to claim crowding. |
| **Supplying crafters or turn-ins** | Traverse from gatherable or craftable inputs into demanded outputs, checking quantities, alternative bundles, unlocks and activity. A popular recipe absorbing six units can outweigh dozens of dormant recipes. Display the quantity embodied in outputs as a demand indicator; self-gathering, existing stock and alternative sources make actual ingredient purchases uncertain. |
| **A changed use or scarce supply** | On an item/recipe/quest change, revisit connected inputs, outputs, farms and held positions, including items with no current listing. On a drop in listed units, compare price-tier movement, observed additions, estimated sales and expiry/cancellation coverage. A missing snapshot cannot trigger a supply shock. |

Group or sort by the decision the player wants to make. Repeatable activities have session gold and g/h; a one-off choice has incremental gold; a hold has capital committed, vendor exit and a catalyst. Use a mixed discovery list to surface new evidence and filter by those purposes. Store the reason a row first appeared, what changed since, and which observations would promote or retire it. Replays must use only information available by the historical decision time.

### Playable returns and realistic quantities

- **Repeated farms.** For each run record route revision, character, elapsed minutes, haul by item, incidental coin and expenses. Support low-friction web entry for time and loot and a personal run history. Include travel/reset time once at the appropriate level. PvP interruptions, failed clears and competition count towards elapsed time. Show duration-weighted returns, run count, range and the share dependent on rare drops. Compute projected g/h as (looted coin + vendor proceeds + scenario net AH proceeds − incremental costs) / total elapsed hours. Split observed cash from unsold output. When a tracked sale arrives, replace that portion of the projection with the receipt in the realised view. Known bound boss loot can contribute vendor proceeds when vendorable. An advertised spawn count alone yields no g/h figure.
- **Tonight’s route vs an hour of repetition.** For a 30-minute session, subtract one-time approach time, fit complete loops in the remainder and apply known cooldown/reset constraints. An 8-minute circuit can have a poor half-hour return if the first journey takes 20 minutes. If a Forever instance limit is unverified, label sustained g/h as conditional or use genuinely elapsed back-to-back sessions.
- **Price change vs player efficiency.** Reprice the *same haul and time* against comparable past/current local prices to measure market-driven change. Compare measured units/hour separately for changes in route performance. Record the baseline/window and coverage. Freeze ownership, route, fee basis and eligibility for a like-for-like comparison, or show why it is unavailable.
- **Sparse routes.** Without personal samples, use an attributable benchmark with its conditions, an editable scenario, or “yield unknown”. Display what yield would beat a chosen alternative. Example: at 50s net per herb, 20 herbs/hour are needed to match a 10g/hour alternative before extra expenses. A single lucky epic does not set the baseline for an entire zone. Keep measured, benchmark and scenario layers separately selectable.
- **Incremental crafts.** Walk the cheapest available asks for the selected input quantities. Cap the quote at observed depth. For owned tradeable inputs, compare the craft with the net raw-sale alternative. For missing inputs, include their batch purchase cost. Include vendor bundle size, stock, character-adjusted purchase prices and outlays that are refundable or lost. Show output ask as a sale scenario, plus observed turnover and competing volume. Deduct a lost deposit once. A refunded deposit recovers an outlay. Partial fills can return part of it. [Observed fees and partial refunds](https://wowfah.com/guides/wow-forever-auction-house-cut-and-deposit)
- **Unlocks and conversions.** Keep both monetary cost and scarce currency visible. When marginal benefit per sale is positive and the batch economics remain applicable, show the smallest whole number of sales needed to cover the incremental unlock cost. Recompute by tier when the margin changes. Show no finite break-even when the available profitable volume cannot repay it. Account for mutually exclusive rewards, first-only and daily gates, guaranteed vs random output, and character-specific eligibility. A reagent consumed by many recipes is a lead; show which outputs have buyers and what their activity can actually establish.

**Worked craft fixture.** For a potion requiring two Wintersbite, two Firebloom and one vial, suppose Wintersbite has two units at 10s and eighteen at 18s, Firebloom is 4s with enough depth, a vial is 1s and the output is offered at 35s. Assume one output per craft, a 5% cut and no lost deposit. The first all-purchased craft nets **4s 25c**. Buying for ten costs **4g 34s** against **3g 32s 50c** in conditional net proceeds, a **1g 1s 50c loss**. If you already have the four herbs for one craft, the 28s gross raw sale is worth 26s 60c net. Crafting and buying the vial leaves 32s 25c, an improvement of **5s 65c**. These prices are synthetic. [Mender’s Potion inputs](https://www.foreverdb.app/en/forever/items/250946/mender-s-potion)

### Three other kinds of lead

**The quest choice.** An Alcaz Document Box can be vendored for 25g or handed in. The Alliance hand-in grants a tradeable Fractured Insignia plus reported gold and XP. Six fragments make a bound toy. At a hypothetical 16g fragment ask and a 1g 60s hand-in reward, vending the box then buying one fragment leaves the same fragment plus **7g 40s more cash** than the hand-in. Show XP forgone, alternative box AH exit if evidenced, quest eligibility and the actual cost of the remaining fragments. For someone who already has the fragment, the box’s 25g exit is in the past. Value the final toy as a personal collection goal. [Wowhead walkthrough](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/alcaz-operative-disguise-toy-secret-walkthrough) · [Recorded Alliance reward](https://ahledger.com/wow-forever/quests/96377-state-secrets) · [Binding and box value](https://wowclassicforever.info/quests/alcaz-operative-disguise/)

**The overlooked BoE.** Robes of Insight, item 940, has a Forever effect reducing spell mana costs by 200 for 12 seconds on a 10-minute cooldown. Compare that effect with substitutes and observed listings. Display the historical Classic tooltip separately. A skilled healer’s burst window supplies a plausible use case. Thin trading, an isolated low ask and future encounters cannot establish a fair resale price. For random-suffix resistance gear retain the original variant and binding status. Where the market provider groups by base ID, mark the price as a base-item reference and allow an unpriced watch. [Forever/Classic comparison](https://foreverchanges.pro/item/940/robes-of-insight)

**The cheap crate.** Price the empty crate plus a complete eligible bundle. Track the introductory Shipping Label bonus separately from the ordinary crate payout and the character’s chance of additional Favor. Writs have their own reputation/reward and repeatability rules. For the Mender’s Potion example, the teaching item is **250990**, the output is **250946**, the documented cost is **45 Favor**, and the learning requirement is **130 Alchemy**. One catalog lists Neutral; the recipe tooltip lists skill without a Friendly requirement. The earlier discussion’s Friendly gate lacks adequate support. Verify the vendor’s purchase gate separately from the learning gate and leave unresolved conditions visible. Filling a crate binds it. Price the alternatives before that choice. [Crates and introductory reward](https://aidvir.github.io/wow-forever-favor/favor.html) · [Writs and turn-ins](https://www.60.tools/guides/merchants-favor) · [Teaching item](https://www.foreverdb.app/en/forever/items/250990/recipe-mender-s-potion) · [Catalog standing](https://forever-hub.com/en/crafting)

## 4. Characters, the addon and the ledger

**Eligibility has three states.** Eligible, ineligible with reasons, and unknown with the missing observation. Keep account-level challenge completion separate from the individual character’s perk allocation, recipes, Favor, reputation and professions. The launch class challenges are level **25, 45 and 60 per class**. Primary **non-gathering** professions award challenges at **150, 225 and 300**. An unearned Alchemy challenge can make a useful alt play. Another character reaching an already completed class milestone cannot earn it again. The launch spend cap of 16 is per character. Model that as a versioned rule, alongside effects such as Well Rested, Bartering, Bountiful Harvest and Performance Bonus. Imported runs already include the perks active during those runs. Applying the same yield bonus again would inflate the estimate. [Blizzard’s Legacy rules and perks](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24307383/get-to-know-the-world-of-warcraft-forever-legacy-system)

**Low-friction setup.** Launch the local web app and choose the house. Settings detects plausible installs and offers **Browse folders** and a paste-path fallback. The loopback service supplies a bounded Windows folder browser. Accept a WoW parent or the `_forever_` product directory. Show the resolved product, build/TOC, addon destination, local account selector and permissions before Install/Update. A fresh product may lack `Interface\AddOns`. Validate the product first, then create missing children on install. Launch-era evidence identifies interface **16001** for client 1.60.1. Resolve the installed build at runtime. Installation and data import have separate status. [Forever folder guide](https://foreverbase.org/en/addons/install/) · [Forever addon build reference](https://github.com/prijkes/WoWForeverAddons)

**Capture and import.** The UI-free addon samples on login and relevant events, without opening windows or driving the game. Export account-scoped, versioned records with stable character identity, game build and separate clocks for login, last seen, inventory and each observed bank/profession scope. Handle the distinction between fresh login, UI reload and zoning when the client exposes the flags. `PLAYER_LOGIN` can also fire on reload. Gear links must retain binding and suffix information when exposed. An empty bank can replace a previously populated bank only after a complete, readable bank observation. A closed bank, delayed item details or a partial profession list cannot erase known data. [Event behaviour](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/PLAYER_ENTERING_WORLD) · [Forever inventory implementation](https://github.com/drockzoids-wow/WoW-Forever-Suite/blob/main/src/WoWForeverSuite/Inventory.lua)

**What “last seen” means.** WoW writes SavedVariables on normal logout or UI reload. The backend can import only what reached disk. Settings distinguishes “installed”, “waiting for first export”, “imported at” and “observed in game at”. If a newly installed addon has not loaded, explain the normal client restart/enable step. A missing bank record calls for the player’s next ordinary bank visit. Rested XP is an observed pool beside current XP and the level denominator. An offline projection also needs a recognised perk rank, rest state, elapsed time and a validated rate/cap; preserve the observed value and label the estimate. [SavedVariables lifecycle](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Saved_Variables) · [Forever rested-XP implementation](https://github.com/addohm/nothing-wasted)

**Accounting.** Bags and bank answer “what was last seen here?” Acquisitions and disposals answer “what did it cost and what was realised?” Keep the two records distinct. Use FIFO within the chosen local ownership pool and item variant for known acquisition lots, retain unknown-basis quantities and unresolved lot order, report only the known share of gain when a disposal includes them, and preserve cost through verified alt transfers. A manual opening balance can attach basis to existing items without creating more physical units. Record manual purchases/sales now. Recognisable purchase/sale invoices observed during normal gameplay can fill the same event schema when the fields are validated. Use a stable receipt identity where possible; ambiguous matches await reconciliation. Returned mail, posting an item, spending it in a craft and moving it to an alt can all reduce bags without a sale. Expose stale overlap across characters, unobserved mail/auctions and mixed timestamps in the position detail. Separate last observed stock from transactions after that observation; the next import reconciles them once. [Forever ledger methodology and sale receipts](https://wowfah.com/about)

**Public accounting fixture.** With 10,000 synthetic units acquired at 1s, entry and vendor exit are each **100g**. In one 500-unit settlement at 3s, a 5% cut leaves **14g 25s**. The known basis of those units is 5g, giving **9g 25s** realised gain before any prior losses. 9,500 units remain economically held. Before a sale this is a scenario. During an export lag the observed inventory can still show 10,000, with a dated reconciliation entry beside it.

**Joining data.** Every derived result carries house, source, item identity and resolution, observation time, method, capacity/coverage, fee and formula version. Buy quotes use a coherent snapshot, or disclose partial or mismatched observations and suppress a current margin. An item absent from a complete full-house snapshot has zero listings at that time and no quoted ask. A failed download yields unknown state. Retain earlier observations at their original times. A historical summary can backfill its own fields only. Compare disjoint windows when estimating trends; summing overlapping 24-hour volumes would count the same trades repeatedly.

**Guide exposure in practice.** Icy Veins recommends a Ghost Mushroom detour at **Skulk Rock** during Herbalism levelling. That supports a named-route exposure marker and says something about likely gatherers. It provides no audience count, sale receipt or measurement of crowding. Show a dated pre/post comparison with the same house, window lengths, observed-hour counts and method. An alternative source of the herb shares the market price, but only the named route receives that article tag. A private instance can face sale-price pressure without competition for its individual nodes. For a guide saying “buy” or “craft”, link downstream demand and upstream reagents accordingly. If there is no usable pre-period, display the advice and its date without a measured response. [Icy Veins route](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/herbalism-leveling-guide)

Optional AI extracts short summaries, the recommended action, specific entities, links and uncertain matches from material available for that use. Store article revision, model, prompt version and source links. A model gets article context and a candidate entity list. Player holdings and account data stay local. Arithmetic, dates, book quantities and game-rule gates come from deterministic records. The research panel remains useful with metadata and manual tags when a model or article is unavailable.

## 6. Local architecture and operations

Use a typed modular application. A React/Vite frontend renders FlexLayout tabsets, SVG geometry, TanStack tables/queries and ECharts. A local Node/Fastify service serves the built assets and typed HTTP endpoints, schedules jobs, and streams updates through one shared SSE connection per browser tab. SQLite stores the market archive within its retention policy, character snapshots, ledger, route observations, job runs and versioned workspace layouts. The development plan specifies the current version baseline and Windows verification. Adding an overlay, scanner rule, panel or source adapter should have an identifiable module and contract; avoid a general plugin execution system for the first release.

Every runtime script and background operation goes through the same runner: market and recipe fetches, guide intake and AI summaries, SavedVariables import/watching, addon installation, analysis, migrations and maintenance. Status shows running/queued/failed/paused, current step, last attempt **and** last success, timestamps, rows processed, retry/backoff, next scheduled time, run ID and duration. Console filters persisted logs by job, run, source and severity, follows live output, replays after reconnect and exposes safe typed controls such as Retry. If the DB is unavailable, a small bootstrap diagnostic and bounded local log explain the failure. A disconnected browser shows last contact. If the host process has exited, the launcher is the recovery entry point.

A Windows launcher starts one service for the local user and gives the browser URL. Closing a tab leaves collection running while that service is alive. PC sleep, a stopped service or provider downtime can interrupt collection. Restart reconciles what a source can genuinely recover. Store local configuration, credentials, backups and player data outside the public checkout. Enforce loopback access, same-origin checks, bounded folder operations and redaction. Keep Demo in a separate store and label it throughout the UI.

Development and verification use **no computer control**. The agents exercise local files, HTTP contracts, rendering logic and an isolated addon harness. The player can inspect actual appearance and a natural in-game export during approval of a reviewed milestone for `main`.

**Release outcome.** An unconfigured player can explore sourced routes and set up the house or addon through the frontend. Once data is available, the player can discover an unwatched peculiarity, compare two playable routes, identify the character that can act, and see how much of the projected return is cash, unsold inventory or speculation.
